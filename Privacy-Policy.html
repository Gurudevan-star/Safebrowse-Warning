Privacy Policy — MailGuard Forensics Last Updated: October 4, 2026 MailGuard Forensics is a browser extension designed to help users identify potentially suspicious or phishing-related signals in emails viewed through supported webmail services. 1. Information Accessed MailGuard Forensics operates on supported webmail pages, currently Gmail and Outlook Web. When an email is visible in the browser, the extension may access the visible message text in order to identify patterns that may be associated with phishing, impersonation, credential requests, payment requests, or other suspicious email characteristics. 2. How Information Is Used The visible email content is processed locally within the browser by the extension. The information is used solely to identify explainable suspicious-email signals and display a warning to the user. Examples of signals include: • Urgency or pressure-related language • Requests for passwords, OTPs, verification codes, or recovery phrases • Requests for certain payment methods • Links combined with account or credential-related requests • Potential brand impersonation indicators 3. Data Transmission MailGuard Forensics does not send email message content to a remote server or third-party service. The current version performs its analysis locally within the user's browser. 4. Data Collection MailGuard Forensics does not intentionally collect or sell personal information. The extension does not intentionally collect: • Passwords • Authentication credentials • Payment information • Names • Email addresses • Email accounts • Contact lists The extension processes visible email content only for the purpose of providing its security-warning functionality. 5. Data Storage The current version does not intentionally store email message content on a remote server. The extension may request browser extension storage permission for extension functionality and future configuration features. No email message content is intentionally stored through a remote service. 6. Data Sharing MailGuard Forensics does not sell, rent, or share email message content with advertisers or other third parties. The current version does not transmit email content to external services. 7. User Control When a potentially suspicious email is detected, MailGuard Forensics displays the reasons for the warning. The user remains in control and can review the message or dismiss the warning. The extension does not prevent the user from accessing or using their email. 8. Security MailGuard Forensics is designed to minimize data access and perform its current analysis locally within the browser. Users should continue to follow normal security practices and independently verify suspicious emails. 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. The latest version of this policy will be available at the publicly accessible URL associated with MailGuard Forensics. 10.
